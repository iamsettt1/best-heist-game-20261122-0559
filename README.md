Midnight Vault: The Halcyon Job — a top-down 2D stealth heist game. You are Wren, a thief crossing the Halcyon Museum of Antiquities at night: read the guards' vision cones, slip through shadows and vents, dodge sweeping cameras, pick the lock on the Star of Halcyon, and reach the skylight before the alarm hits 100. Three nights, three rooms, one getaway.
## How to play
- **WASD / arrows** — move. **Shift** — sneak (slower, harder to spot).
- **E** — pick a lock, grab loot, or crawl a vent (hold still while it channels).
- **Click** — toss a coin to distract a nearby guard. **Q** — EMP pulse: disables cameras for 6s but spikes the alarm.
- **P** — pause. **M** — mute. **R** — restart. Idle for 8 seconds and the autopilot takes over.
- Steal the Star, then reach the glowing exit. Stay out of the red cones, or the alarm climbs — at 100 the museum locks down.
## Run it
```sh
node serve.ts
```
Then open http://localhost:8080
